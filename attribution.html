Project snapshots assembled 18 October 2026. Research previews preserve original outcomes, including failures. Project summaries are available in English, Persian and Danish; original reports remain in English.

The portfolio and recent project work were developed with OpenAI Codex assistance. This does not imply independent authorship, production deployment, employment results or real-world validation.

Streetwise uses KITTI imagery (Geiger et al.), CC BY-NC-SA 3.0. Noncommercial research display only; the original attribution is retained. Solar images and other third-party data retain their original terms, linked in each repository.

The browser hand simulation uses Three.js, MIT: license. Its motion is synthetic; no camera or model service is running. Hand-landmarker weights and the external sample photograph are not redistributed.

No custom analytics scripts, tracking cookies, contact form, paid APIs or server-side inference are included. The language preference is saved only in the visitor’s browser. Hosting providers may collect visitor statistics and maintain access logs. Contact links open external providers.
