Method, accounting and limitations
  • Data: Energinet Day-Ahead Prices, 1 October 2025–10 October 2026 exclusive in Danish local time. Four equal quarter-hour prices form an hourly average. Negative prices are retained; incomplete hours and days are not filled.
  • Snapshot history: 1 July–28 September 2026, 90 complete UTC days. The simulated issue date is 30 September; the last included hour finishes 24 hours before issue. Delivery begins 48 hours after issue.
  • Overlapping seven-day blocks preserve within-week temporal and cross-zone relationships. Every start day receives equal weight, so interior dates occur in more blocks than boundary dates. No random samples are added and no seasonal matching is performed.
  • The 95th-percentile cost uses the inverse empirical CDF. Expected shortfall is the mean of the worst 5% probability mass: 4.2 of 84 equally weighted scenarios, with fractional boundary weight. Contributions use the same portfolio-tail weights and sum to portfolio expected shortfall.
  • These are total procurement-cost statistics, not trading loss, return, or margin estimates. Expected shortfall is used as an allocation objective on the same historical sample; its improvement is in-sample.
  • Hourly demand shapes are synthetic and repeat daily. Fixed-price contracts exactly follow the hedged share of those shapes. Actual contract availability, consumption deviations, liquidity, transaction charges, financing and counterparty risk are not modeled.
  • Fixed-price settlement benefit = hedged share of spot cost − hedged MWh × fixed price. Procurement cost = unhedged spot cost − benefit. The expected uplift budget does not cap adverse settlement or collateral needs.
  • Historical prices may be revised; the public API is not a publication-vintage archive. This retrospective project was built in October 2026, not actually traded at the simulated issue dates.
  • The rolling check shows undercoverage. Joint historical replay cannot by itself anticipate new price regimes or extremes beyond the observed sample.
